Morse Training: Learn Morse


Learn Morse code with games and in-screen or external paddles





Perfect your Morse code (CW) timing and technique with Morse Training.

Whether you are an Amateur Radio enthusiast or a beginner learning the language of dits and dahs (dots and dashes), Morse Training offers a professional-grade interface to improve your skills.

Key Features:
- Free text mode, Transmit game, and Receive game: Practice your way.
- Versatile Input Support: Use your keyboard, touch screen, mouse, or connect a physical paddle via a USB adapter for the most authentic experience. 
- Keyer Modes: Supports Straight, Iambic A and B, Ultimatic, Bug, and Cootie keyers.
-  Configurable Timing: Possibility to adjust inter-letter and inter-word timing, with an option for Strict Mode that challenges you with proper, standard timing between letters and words, perfect for those aiming for professional proficiency.
- Advanced Hardware Integration: Unique support for USB-to-mouse and USB-to-keyboard paddle adapters lets you use your actual telegraphy gear with your Android device.

This app is free and contains no ads.
